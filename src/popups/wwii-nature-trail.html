WWII Nature Trail

This land is owned and maintained by the Natural Resources Trust of Mansfield.

Adjusted from their website:

WWII Veterans Memorial Nature Trail is located 1/4 mile from the Branch street entrance to the WWII Veterans Memorial Trail and 3/4 of a mile from Fruit Street. Best parking is at the Fruit Street entrance. The parcel has nearly a mile of trails in good condition. A portion of the trails goes along Back Bay Brook and circumvents a wetland area.
